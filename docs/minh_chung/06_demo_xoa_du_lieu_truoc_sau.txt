=== KIEM CHUNG XOA DU LIEU CA NHAN TRUOC - SAU QUA API (TAI KHOAN THU NGHIEM) ===
1) Truoc khi xoa: GET /api/v1/user/privacy/export-data/
   Kieu du lieu tra ve: JSON gom cac mang scans[], chat_sessions[], reports[], alerts[]
   So phan tu trong cac mang: len(scans)=1, len(chat_sessions)=1, len(alerts)=1
   scans[0].scan_type=message, chat_sessions[0].title=Phien tu van thu nghiem

2) Gui yeu cau xoa chon loc: POST /api/v1/user/privacy/delete-data/
   Payload: {"scopes": ["scans", "chat_sessions", "alerts"]}
   HTTP Status: 200
   Response JSON: {"deleted": {"scans": 1, "chat_sessions": 1, "alerts": 1}, "scopes": ["alerts", "chat_sessions", "scans"], "message": "Đã xóa dữ liệu đã chọn khỏi tài khoản."}

3) Truy van lai sau khi xoa: GET /api/v1/user/privacy/export-data/
   Mang tra ve: scans=[], chat_sessions=[], alerts=[]
